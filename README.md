# Adjusted Net Income Calculator UK — The Figure That Actually Decides Things

> Adjusted net income, not your salary, decides the personal allowance taper, the Child Benefit charge and childcare eligibility.

Reference notes for the UK tax year **2026/27**, computed from HMRC's
published rates rather than copied from another site.

## How it works

HMRC's definition, in order:

1. Total taxable income from all sources: salary, benefits in kind, rental profit, savings interest, dividends
2. **Minus** pension contributions that got relief at source, grossed up
3. **Minus** Gift Aid donations, grossed up
4. **Minus** trading losses

The result is adjusted net income.

This is why someone on a £105,000 salary can have adjusted net income of £95,000 and keep their full personal allowance, and why someone on £95,000 with a company car can be over £100,000 without realising.

## What this does not cover

Salary sacrifice reduces it because the money was never your income. A net-pay pension contribution already reduced your taxable pay, so do not subtract it twice.

## Source and tax year

Figures are for the 2026/27 tax year, which runs 6 April 2026
to 5 April 2027, and were checked against
[gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027) on 28 August 2026.

Rates change every April, and often at a Budget. Check the tax year a figure
belongs to before relying on it.
